# Exploratory R&D-to-market-value portfolio comparison

**Fixed before this calculation; exploratory, not an independently preregistered test.** Compare the published `RD` portfolio `05`, the highest R&D expense relative to market equity, with French broad-market total return. This question is economically related to value: a low share price can increase the ratio. Preserve the earlier value-factor NO-GO and do not treat this as an independent replication.

Use the Open Source Asset Pricing 2026.10 original-construction portfolio file (SHA-256 `76599d94beab94cc8973d8a126e8efbed98ee79f677738c55b2389f17387ba96`) and the previously frozen French market-factor ZIP (SHA-256 `b03d39e4bb253dda0f14893db3b78e64b40b8d6549a0fc0fd4111dae84c98618`). Provider metadata specifies equal weights, sign +1, 20% tails and annual June assignments. The provider's published data and French market have different vintages. The high portfolio is broad-market, not an S&P 500 stock list; monthly equal weighting does not let winners grow unchecked.

Compare July 1975–December 2025 and January 1994–December 2025. July 1975 is within the paper's original sample and annual June formation convention; the end is the data release's documented end. Apply 1% annual cost as a monthly subtraction to the candidate, plus a 2% stress; market has no corresponding haircut. Compute 12-lag Newey–West t and CAGR difference, presenting the existing t ≥ 3 / +1 percentage point since-1994 standard as context, not a confirmed pass. Costs are assumptions, tax and next-session execution are unmodeled.

The original paper's result, prior factor results and a multi-signal data preview were visible before choosing this idea. The portfolio file had already been downloaded and read for another screen. The 1994 window overlaps the original paper's sample. Therefore there is no clean independent timing or cross-study multiplicity control; a favorable result is a lead for further research, not proof or authorization. Missing data, duplicate months or invalid returns stop the calculation. No outcome changes production, prior verdicts or the paper-trader freeze.
