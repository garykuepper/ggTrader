### 4. [MEDIUM-HIGH — Confirmed Defect] Disproportionate & Self-Contradictory Data-Quality Stops in `§9`
* **Evidence:** [`2026-10-24-intraday-feasibility.md:123`](briefs/2026-10-24-intraday-feasibility.md#L123) halts the entire 10.7-year study as `UNRESOLVED (data)` if *any* regular-session minute (out of ~1,044,810 minutes across 2,679 offline-calendar full sessions) is missing without a verified exchange-halt record—even though the strategy only ever touches `P_close(t-1)`, `09:59`, `15:30`, and `P_exit`, and the last sentence of the same bullet contradicts the first by defining session eligibility solely by whether "every endpoint price in §4 can be built exactly." Similarly, [`:125`](briefs/2026-10-24-intraday-feasibility.md#L125) requires explaining every close discrepancy across ~2,679 sessions unless attributable to trade conditions, corporate actions, or vendor revisions.
* **Quant Assessment:** Requiring 100% interior-minute completeness (e.g., 11:42 ET) and manual forensic explanations for every `15:59`-vs-daily-close discrepancy guarantees a false `UNRESOLVED (data)` halt on irrelevant noise.
  * **However, a critical quant caveat on missingness rules:** If `09:59` is missing on day $t$, a live trader knows it at 10:00 ET and stays flat ($s_t = 0$, no lookahead). **What if `09:59` and `15:30` are present ($r_{\text{first}} > 0$, so the trader enters at 15:30), and only the exit bar (`15:59` or auction) is missing?** Treating a missing exit on an active trade day as a "no-trade day" ($s_t = 0$) would condition entry on future data availability (**lookahead bias**). Therefore, `r8` must distinguish:
    1. *Pre-entry missingness* (`P_close(t-1)` or `09:59` or `15:30` missing): can be treated as a logged no-trade day (`s_t = 0`) up to a strict pre-registered cap.
    2. *Post-entry missingness* (`P_exit` missing when `P_close(t-1)`, `09:59`, and `15:30` are present): must either trigger `UNRESOLVED (data)` immediately (if count $> 0$) or use a strictly defined deterministic fallback (such as the session's last available regular-grid bar close or daily bar close) without dropping the trade.
* **Comparison with Opus (`F7`):** **Agree on restricting binding validation to endpoint prices and dropping interior-minute/blanket-reconciliation halts, but add the necessary pre-entry vs. post-entry missingness distinction above** so "treating an ineligible session as a no-trade day" ([`opus-review.md:83`](2026-10-25-intraday-overall-plan-opus-review.md#L83)) cannot introduce lookahead when an exit bar is missing.

### 7. [MEDIUM — Conditional Statistical Context] Power, Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE), `A1` Attribution, and Multiple-Testing Reuse
* **Evidence & Verified Bounds:**
  * Under the conditional planning assumptions in [`2026-10-25-intraday-opus-estimates-verification.md:9-48`](2026-10-25-intraday-opus-estimates-verification.md#L9-L48) ($N = 2,670$ full sessions, assumed whole-calendar daily $\sigma = 25\text{ bp}$, 50% trade frequency, 2.0 bps base round-trip cost), crossing $t = 3.0$ at **~50% nominal power** requires **$1.451462\text{ bp/session}$ net** ($2.902924\text{ bp/trade}$ net, $4.902924\text{ bp/trade}$ gross). Achieving **80% or 90% normal IID power** requires **$1.858656\text{ bp/session}$** or **$2.071503\text{ bp/session}$ net** ($5.7173\text{ bp}$ or $6.1430\text{ bp/trade}$ gross).
  * Furthermore, Gao et al. (2018) Table 6 reports $6.21\%$ annualized ($\approx 39.1193\text{ bp/session}$) for the *always-long* last half-hour, which does **not** identify the conditional variance of the long-only rule ($\text{Var}(I \cdot R) = q E[R^2 | I=1] - q^2 E[R | I=1]^2$; a rough $q=0.5$ equal-second-moment proxy is $27.6615\text{ bp}$, not $25\text{ bp}$), nor does IID normal power measure the actual 5-lag Bartlett Newey–West (`HAC`) gate or joint `A1 & A2` pass probability.
  * On `A1` meaning ([`2026-10-24-intraday-feasibility.md:89-91`](briefs/2026-10-24-intraday-feasibility.md#L89-L91)): Because $E[d \cdot R] = \text{Cov}(d, R) + E[d]E[R]$, an intercept-only mean test on $s_t$ conflates predictive timing ($\text{Cov}(d, R) > 0$) with passive exposure to unconditional afternoon drift ($E[R] > 0$).
  * On multiple testing ([`2026-10-24-intraday-feasibility.md:73`](briefs/2026-10-24-intraday-feasibility.md#L73)): Alpaca provides only one free 2016–2026 SIP sample. Any subsequent intraday candidates (e.g., parked Zarattini noise bands or ORB in [`:134`](briefs/2026-10-24-intraday-feasibility.md#L134)) will reuse this exact window.
* **Comparison with Opus (`F2`, `F10`, `F11`):**
  * **Disagree with Opus (`F2`) calling $1.5\text{ bp/day}$ the "minimum detectable effect"** without specifying power: $1.451\text{ bp/day}$ is the 50%-power threshold-crossing point under assumed IID $\sigma=25\text{ bp}$, whereas 80%/90% power requires $1.86\text{–}2.07\text{ bp/day}$ net (plus any positive serial-correlation HAC inflation). **Agree**, however, that no new threshold should be manufactured and that `r8` should include this qualified conditional planning note so the owner approves `A1`/`A2` with clear eyes.
  * **Agree with Opus (`F10`)** on labeling the window *"post-original-sample, single-shot"* (not an untouched holdout, given public 2014–2026 replications), logging a cumulative intraday hypothesis counter $K$ on the 2016–2026 tape ($K=1$ here), and forbidding diagnostic subperiods/tables from parameterizing future briefs—while noting ([`estimates-verification.md:91-97`](2026-10-25-intraday-opus-estimates-verification.md#L91-L97)) that a counter documents reuse without controlling family-wise error under adaptive selection unless a formal multi-study Holm/Bonferroni adjustment is later adopted.
  * **Agree with Opus (`F11`)** that `A1` can remain a single profitability gate ($t \ge 3.0$) rather than adding a second hurdle, provided `r8` pre-registers the attribution diagnostic interpretation (comparing net long-only vs. net always-long last-half-hour and reporting the NW $t$-stat on $\beta_1$ in $r_{\text{last}, t} = \alpha + \beta_1 \mathbb{I}[r_{\text{first}, t} > 0] + \epsilon_t$).
