# Window use for stock-selection research

**As of 2026-11-28.** A window is **spent** when it has already shaped or scored a rule
and cannot give a clean test of a new rule tuned after seeing it. A **development window**
is the period used to build a rule or choose settings. A **holdout window** is set aside
until one final scoring. A **fresh window** is a period whose results for the specified
rule were unseen until it was scored once. Prices read to calculate the first picks also
count as used inputs. A **rolling test** chooses settings on an earlier slice and scores
them on a later slice, then moves both slices forward. A **fold** is one pair of
training and test slices in a rolling test. Dates and roles below are as reported. “Not established” means the report leaves the detail open.

CRSP is the university stock-price source. **Combined prices** are the first published
database version joining CRSP to Yahoo prices where available. The dead-stock studies
use CRSP import 1 for stock details. **Dead stocks** are stocks whose trading histories
ended. SPY is the fund that tracks the S&P 500. **Legacy prices** means the older
database-first Yahoo price path, which could get missing prices from another provider.
Exact provider coverage for each older run is not established. The loader is
`src/ggTrader/lab/data.py` in the saved code version tagged
`legacy-engine-vbt0285`. See the
[dated database inventory](../../docs/database-inventory-2026-11-26.md#in-the-database).

  1998-07-31 on 2026-11-28, so that span is now spent for the first test's rule and its
  yearly and jump-skipping variants. Only 1968 and August 1998 through 1999 were not scored
  (the last stretch was already used as input by earlier studies). Before that run, no
  individual-stock account score was found on these dates in the reviewed reports. The early momentum test read from August 1998
  for its first picks.
  The random comparison, momentum death audit and early filing study rebuilt the same
  early picks or stock list using those earlier inputs. The integrity study counted
  rows for one stock from 1992 onward. The early filing study also searched 1999 SEC
  lists. Published portfolio comparisons above examined returns from this era. Source
  coverage starting in 1968 is not a score. Before August 1998, no rule input use was
  found in the stock study reports, with the earlier row-count inspection disclosed
  above. The held CRSP copy includes stocks that later stopped trading and has prices,
  returns and shares outstanding, so it can support a historical test of price and
  company-size rules. It lacks trading volume and delisting returns, the payout or
  final loss when a stock leaves the market. It cannot show how many shares could
  have traded or what investors actually received at removal. The database's SPY
  prices start on 2000-01-03, with a separate frozen table covering 1993-01-29 to
  1999-12-31; before 1993 the early-decades study used the CRSP market return as a stand-in. See the [inventory](../../docs/database-inventory-2026-11-26.md#data-not-available-for-free),
  [fresh-window report, §2](../stock-signals/dead-stock-fresh-window/2026-11-24-dead-stock-fresh-window.md#2-what-was-done),
  [random comparison, §2](../stock-signals/dead-stock-random-baseline/2026-11-25-dead-stock-random-baseline.md#2-what-was-done),
  [momentum death audit, brief §2](../stock-signals/dead-stock-momentum-deaths/2026-11-25-dead-stock-momentum-deaths-brief.md#2-data-and-freeze-before-calculation),
  [early filing study, §2](../stock-signals/dead-stock-early-filings/2026-11-25-dead-stock-early-filings.md#2-what-was-done)
  and [integrity hand checks](../stock-signals/dead-stock-data-integrity/2026-11-24-dead-stock-data-integrity.md#earlier-measurements-and-checks-made-by-hand).
- **Yahoo, January 2025 to 2026-11-05.** This period is partly spent, and the held
  data leave no clean unused window for individual-stock rules. The earlier July
  stock studies, momentum replays, stock-model reruns, sizing tests and stock
  model plus SPY comparison used parts of it. The S&P 500 fallback check used data
  through 2026-08-18 to choose settings. The engine and membership checks replayed
  buy and sell decisions through 2026-10-30. The September and November paper
  reviews examined the individual-stock holdings and compared the paper account
  against SPY. The November review covered the 2026-11-03 close and separately
  used the 2026-11-04 account snapshot. The report locators are in the table.

  A read-only database count on 2026-11-26 checked the Yahoo daily rows
  (`venue='yfinance'`, `interval='1d'`) in `public.ohlcv` and the saved copy
  `market.yahoo_daily_frozen_20261126`. After 2026-10-30, the only days with normal coverage
  were 2026-11-02, 2026-11-03 and 2026-11-04, each with 623 symbols. All three
  fall within the November paper review. The last date, 2026-11-05, has partial
  coverage of 18 symbols. A forward test would need a new, later period.

  The held Yahoo collection has 1,443 symbols across its full history. Its
  2025–2026 coverage is limited to survivors, stocks that did not stop trading.
  It can show how a rule would have behaved on the covered stocks. The full
  market would also include stocks missing from this collection when they stopped
  trading. The combined-price momentum runs ended on 2024-12-31. Their saved
  records also identify the Yahoo data version by its newer dates, and the payout
  and open-cases reports used early-2025 SEC filings. See the
  [current inventory](../../docs/database-inventory-2026-11-26.md#in-the-database)
  and the [November 23 inventory, Yahoo count](../../docs/archive/database-inventory-2026-11-23.md).
