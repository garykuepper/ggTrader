## External and mathematical claims

8. **SPIVA counts — verified in [S&P DJI's primary table, report 1a, p. 12](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/spiva/spiva-us-year-end-2025.pdf).** All large-cap funds versus S&P 500: 85.59%, 89.93% and 92.89% underperformed over 10, 15 and 20 years respectively, ending 2025-12-31. Category-level historical fund results are not a predicted probability for ggTrader, a paper account or an individual manager. These are fund/index comparisons, not an after-tax retail replication.
9. **Sharpe estimation and annualization — supported by [Lo (2002)](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/research/financial-analysts-journal/2002/the-statistics-of-sharpe-ratios).** The paper addresses estimation uncertainty, serial correlation and restrictions on square-root-of-time annualization. Its hedge-fund example is not a numeric correction factor for ggTrader.
10. **Stop-order execution — supported by [SEC investor bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-15) and [FINRA Notice 16-19](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/notice_other_file_ref/Regulatory-Notice-16-19.pdf).** A stop-market order can execute away from the trigger; a stop-limit order may not execute. A daily OHLC bar does not generally identify the sequence of multiple intraday touches; that is an inference from the information available in a bar, not an observed test result.
11. **EDGAR — supported by [SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).** Free endpoints contain submission history and XBRL data with documented coverage restrictions. The source does not establish that a fully point-in-time, revision-aware, survivorship-safe investment dataset is available without further reconstruction.
12. **SPY inception — verified from [State Street](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-spdr-sp-500-etf-trust-spy): 1993-01-22.** Earlier index histories are not the same as actual SPY fund returns.
13. **Deflated Sharpe attribution — verified by [authors' paper](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf).** The unrelated arXiv link in one AI report is not the original paper. No claim is made that this paper independently validates any project's DSR gate.
14. **IR illustration — checked arithmetic.** 0.01/0.04 = 0.25; 0.01/0.06 ≈ 0.1667; 0.02/0.06 ≈ 0.3333; 0.02/0.04 = 0.50. Valid only for commensurate annual arithmetic active return and tracking error, not a CAGR conversion.
15. **Sample-size illustration — derived, not an empirical observation.** Paired arithmetic differences have standard error based on the variability of paired differences ([NIST paired t-test](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman1/auxillar/t_test.htm)). Under independent normal observations, known constant annual standard deviation, a normal two-sided α=0.05 test and 80% power, the standard approximation uses `(1.96 + 0.842)^2 ≈ 7.85`; with TE=2% and mean active return=1%, n ≈ 31.4 independent years. This is approximate, not the exact finite-sample t-test, a timing guarantee, or a CAGR inference. The report now labels the normal approximation explicitly. Dependence, selection, regimes and costs need separate assessment.
16. **Tax conclusions — deliberately limited.** [IRS Publication 550](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550) discusses investment income, basis, gains, losses, wash sales and other situations. No report-specific lot ledger or tax calculation was supplied sufficient to verify the questioned liquidation benefit or break-even hurdle. Neither is adopted. Tax laws and personal circumstances require separate review; no personal rates are asserted.
17. **Report input identity — hashes verified.** The four T3 attachments were hashed when the synthesis was written; the digest list identifies bytes only, not accuracy. The source files themselves are not in this repository and may not persist with a clone. The AI reports' every underlying claim was not audited.

## Proposals, interpretations, and remaining gaps

The recommended objective, benchmark set, sequencing, and attribution inventory are **proposals**, not externally verified facts or owner-approved rules. Identifying stocks programmatically, earning positive returns in downturns, and controlling losses are research hypotheses; no performance claim follows from the sources above. Technical claims about prospective self-financing, lookahead controls, matching and lot accounting are methodological requirements that need a detailed brief and implementation tests, not measurements. The inventory of underlying replica position and cash records has **not** been performed; feasibility and causal decomposition remain unresolved. A broad claim that the whole project is free of bias or that all source citations in the four AI reports are sound has not been checked.
