# Trading books and papers library

A separate collection of trading books, papers, monographs and lectures for ggTrader research. The references below were retrieved 2026-11-12. These works provide background and methods; they do not establish a trading edge or replace point-in-time, out-of-sample tests against the relevant benchmark. Verify assumptions, costs and applicability before using a method.

When adding a work, save its PDF here when available and record its title, author, source, retrieval date and SHA-256 below. Link existing copies held in another library rather than duplicating them. Keep unavailable works clearly marked.

The PDFs are local reference copies, excluded from git; check rights before redistribution. The source links below allow retrieval on another machine. SHA-256 hashes identify the downloaded copies, not the validity of their contents. This collection is separate from DeepThought's primary-research library and is not indexed by `research library search`.

- [Algorithmic Trading and Computational Finance](fundamentals/kearns-algorithmic-trading.pdf), Michael Kearns, 2012 tutorial slides. Market structure and execution.
