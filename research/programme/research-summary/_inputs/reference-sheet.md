# Reference sheet for the strategy-category summary

Terms used in the summary, in plain words. The lint reads the first column.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| bps | Basis points. 100 bps is one percentage point a year. |
| t-statistic | A result divided by its estimated uncertainty. Near zero means the result cannot be told from luck. |
| benchmark | The comparison investment a strategy is measured against. |
| SPY | A fund that tracks the S&P 500, the large US stocks. |
| SSO | A fund that aims for twice the daily move of the S&P 500. |
| MDY | A fund that tracks the S&P MidCap 400. |
| FOMC | The US central bank committee that sets interest rates. |
| R&D | Research and development spending. |
| tax line | About +240 bps a year before tax, the audit's estimate of what a book selling within a year needs to tie SPY after tax. |
| S&P | Standard & Poor's, the index provider. The S&P 500 is 500 large US stocks. |
