  - Data
    - [Data](/features/data): Connect, store, update, transform, and combine local and remote market data

    - [Binance data](/features/data/binance-data): Download Binance spot and futures klines with trade counts and taker volume
    - [Data storage and databases](/features/data/data-storage-and-databases): Save and query market data in files, DuckDB, SQL databases, and ArcticDB
    - [Data pipelines](/features/data/financial-data-pipelines): Align, update, transform, and analyze multi-symbol market data in one object
    - [Multi-timeframe analysis](/features/data/multi-timeframe-analysis): Resample and realign data across timeframes by when each value became available
    - [Synthetic data](/features/data/synthetic-data): Generate reproducible random walks, GBM paths, and synthetic OHLC bars
  - Indicators and signals
    - [Indicators and signals](/features/indicators): Compute indicators, build your own, stream updates, and turn them into signals
    - [Technical indicators](/features/indicators/technical-indicators): Run TA-Lib, pandas-ta, ta, SMC, WorldQuant Alphas, and native indicators at scale
    - [Custom indicators](/features/indicators/indicator-development): Turn Python, pandas, or Numba calculations into reusable indicators with parameter grids
    - [Streaming indicators](/features/indicators/streaming-indicators): Update indicators bar by bar with stateful accumulators that match batch results
    - [Trading signals](/features/indicators/trading-signals): Generate, combine, clean, and analyze entry and exit signals before backtesting
    - [Machine learning labels](/features/indicators/machine-learning-labels): Create triple-barrier, trend, and pivot labels, and backtest model predictions
  - Backtesting
    - [Backtesting](/features/backtesting): Model orders, signals, leverage, stops, limits, cash flows, and simulation callbacks
    - [Backtesting engine](/features/backtesting/backtesting-engine): Simulate orders, signals, and callbacks across many assets and parameters at once
    - [Signal backtesting](/features/backtesting/signal-backtesting): Turn entry and exit signals into long, short, reversing, and pyramided positions
    - [Event-driven backtesting](/features/backtesting/event-driven-backtesting): Write compiled callbacks for cooldowns, position limits, and custom simulators
    - [Live simulation](/features/backtesting/live-simulation): Continue backtests on new bars, chain runs, and feed an external trading system
    - [Intraday and tick backtesting](/features/backtesting/intraday-and-tick-backtesting): Backtest minute bars, ticks, and sessions, and resolve stops on finer data
    - [Realistic backtests](/features/backtesting/backtest-realism): Avoid look-ahead bias, impossible fills, and survivorship bias in your backtests
    - [Orders and execution](/features/backtesting/orders-and-execution): Simulate limit and stop-entry orders, fill prices, timing, rejections, and real fills
    - [Stop loss and take profit](/features/backtesting/stop-loss-and-take-profit): Backtest stop losses, trailing stops, take profits, time stops, and exit ladders
    - [Position sizing](/features/backtesting/position-sizing): Size trades in units, cash, percent of equity, target weights, or risk per trade
    - [Fees, slippage, and leverage](/features/backtesting/fees-slippage-and-leverage): Model commissions, slippage, leverage, futures multipliers, and negative prices
    - [Portfolio accounting](/features/backtesting/portfolio-accounting): Track shared cash, deposits, dividends, positions, records, and exposure per bar
  - Analysis
    - [Analysis](/features/analytics): Measure returns and risk, dissect trades, chart results, and study market events
    - [Performance and risk metrics](/features/analytics/performance-metrics): Compute returns, Sharpe, Sortino, benchmark, and rolling metrics on any backtest
    - [Drawdown analysis](/features/analytics/drawdown-analysis): Measure the depth, duration, and recovery of every drawdown as records
    - [Trade analytics](/features/analytics/trade-analytics): Analyze trades and positions with MAE, MFE, edge ratio, profit factor, and plots
    - [Interactive charts](/features/analytics/interactive-charts): Plot backtests, candlesticks, indicators, signals, and parameter heatmaps with Plotly
    - [Patterns and event studies](/features/analytics/patterns-and-event-studies): Search price history for similar patterns and study what happened after events
    - [Records and mapped arrays](/features/analytics/records-and-mapped-arrays): Store sparse events and intervals as records, then filter, reduce, and regroup them
  - Optimization and validation
    - [Optimization and validation](/features/optimization): Search parameters, validate out of sample, test robustness, and allocate across assets
    - [Parameter optimization](/features/optimization/strategy-optimization): Sweep millions of parameter combinations with grids, conditions, and random search
    - [Walk-forward and cross-validation](/features/optimization/time-series-cross-validation): Test optimized parameters out of sample with rolling, expanding, and purged splits
    - [Robustness and overfitting](/features/optimization/robustness-and-overfitting): Check whether a backtest is luck with baselines, permutation tests, and deflated Sharpe
    - [Portfolio optimization](/features/optimization/portfolio-optimization): Allocate with your own functions or optimizer libraries and backtest rebalancing
  - Strategies
    - [Strategies](/features/strategies): Follow worked strategies that combine data, signals, simulation, and validation end to end
    - [Pairs trading](/features/strategies/pairs-trading): Screen cointegrated pairs, trade spread z-scores with shared cash, and validate them
    - [Cross-sectional strategies](/features/strategies/cross-sectional-strategies): Rank a universe each period, rotate into the leaders, and test factors by quintile
    - [Multi-strategy portfolios](/features/strategies/multi-strategy-portfolios): Stack strategies as one portfolio, weight them, and compare recurring investing plans

    - [Benchmarks](/features/performance/benchmarks): Compare simulators on the same orders and measure each backend on your own machine
