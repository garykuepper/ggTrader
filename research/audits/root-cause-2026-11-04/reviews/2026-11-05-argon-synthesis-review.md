## 1. Bottom Line

The root-cause synthesis (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-root-cause-investigation.md:14-60`) is directionally right on its most important point: across 72 measured tests (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:31-35`), the project has not yet found a stock-picking or cryptocurrency strategy that credibly beats SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (`SPY`) buy-and-hold, let alone doubles `SPY`'s raw cumulative total return (`2x SPY`). In the frozen production paper account, ~85.3% of capital sits in `SPY`, ~5.3% in cash, and only ~9.4% in up to three stocks (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens9-power-and-regime.md:49-54`), making it mathematically impossible for the stock sleeve to move book-level return by the required +9.57 percentage points per year (`pp/yr`) over a 5.24-year window unless the picks beat `SPY` by +102.1 pp/yr (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:196-205`). However, the synthesis's headline table of "0 reliable verdicts out of 114" (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-root-cause-investigation.md:45-58`) is an artifact of self-defeating classification rules and broken walk-forward optimization (`WFO`) circuit-breaker machinery rather than proof that all 53 rejected ideas had zero signal. ## 2. Assessment of the Synthesis

### Where the Core Conclusion Holds Up
The synthesis accurately separates two eras of project history (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-root-cause-investigation.md:20-35`):
1. **Early false positives (Phases 1–12):** In-sample parameter selection on 2023–2025 bull-market winners (`NVDA`, `META`, `MSFT`, `AMZN`, `GOOGL`, `AVGO`) and survivorship-biased universes inflated a 1.12 Sharpe ratio that collapsed to -0.17 out-of-sample (`OOS`) (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens1-backtest-validity.md:19-46`).
2. **Current live architecture:** The production replica (`research/a06_production_replica.py`) trailed `SPY` Compound Annual Growth Rate (`CAGR`) by -0.64 pp/yr on its 2021–2026 primary window (14.41% vs 15.05%, Newey-West lag-5 `NW(5)` $t = -0.69$) and beat `SPY` by +1.00 pp/yr on its 2010–2020 holdout ($t = 0.89$) (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:360-390`). Roughly 0.90 pp/yr of the primary shortfall is the 5.3% uninvested cash reserve; against an exposure-matched passive book (94.7% `SPY` + 5.3% T-bill cash), the replica added +0.26 pp/yr ($t = 0.20$), which is indistinguishable from noise (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-root-cause-investigation.md:82-92`).

### Where I Push Back or Discount the Synthesis

1. **How Lens 2's classification rules manufacture "0 reliable verdicts":**  
   - **Rule R5 power circularity:** Under Lens 2 Rule R5 (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:187-190`), a test is only a "reliable NO" if its Minimum Detectable Effect (`MDE`, defined as $(3.0 + 0.8416) \times \text{Standard Error [SE]}$ at $t \ge 3$ and 80% statistical power) is less than or equal to the *study's own registered economic bar* (+1.0 pp/yr under the 2026-10-23 standard), and only falls back to the `2x SPY` target gap when no bar was registered. Because equity excess returns have high variance, even 99 years of Kenneth French data has an indicative `MDE` of ~2.5 pp/yr (and ~4.4 pp/yr post-1994, `research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:63-71`). Demanding `MDE` $\le 1.0$ pp/yr at $t \ge 3$ makes "reliable NO" mathematically unreachable on 5 to 100 years of stock data. Against the owner's actual `2x SPY` target (+9.57 pp/yr over 2021–2026), the production replica (`MDE` = 4.24 pp/yr at book level) *was* well-powered (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens9-power-and-regime.md:43-54, 396-399`) and reliably ruled out `2x SPY` for that configuration.
   - **JSON key formalism on the French screens:** The three long-history Kenneth French screens (`stocks-french-momentum-1927-2026`, `stocks-french-factor-screen-h1-h4`, `stocks-net-issuance-1963-2026`) were classified as "inconclusive" solely because their saved JSON artifacts recorded $t$-statistics and `CAGR` differences without a top-level arithmetic-mean excess key (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:52-58`)—even though Lens 7 recomputed all three from the raw files on disk and matched every number to $10^{-12}$ (`docs/research/2026-11-04-lens7-reproducibility-and-data.md:80-86`).
   - **Asymmetry between GO and NO-GO rules (R6 vs R5):** Rule R6 does not require a $t$-statistic or power threshold for a "reliable GO" (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:214-224`). Four `GO` rows (`stocks-a23-static-leverage`, `cross-asset-static-80-20-sleeve`, `cross-asset-sleeve-holdout-2007-2011`, and `stocks-wfo-policy-synthetic`) failed *only* Rule R3 (driver code not inspected in Lens 1) and would flip to "reliable GO" once read—even though the 80/20 cross-asset sleeve trailed `SPY` `CAGR` by -1.09 pp/yr (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:217-224`).

2. **Power and Horizon Compounding Arithmetic:**  
   The synthesis notes that doubling `SPY`'s cumulative total return (`2x SPY`) requires +9.57 pp/yr over 2021–2026 (`SPY` +108.60% total return, 15.05% `CAGR` $\to$ target +217.21%, 24.63% `CAGR`), +6.14 pp/yr over 2010–2020, +3.55 pp/yr over 2006–2026, +2.34 pp/yr over 1994–2026, and +0.77 pp/yr over 1927–2026 (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:157-183`). What the synthesis under-explains is *why* the annual gap shrinks: one extra doubling of ending wealth over $T$ years adds approximately $(2^{1/T} - 1) \times (1 + g_{\text{SPY}})$ per year. Over 32.3 years (1994–2026, where the French market multiplied wealth 27.6x), ending with 55.2x instead of 27.6x only takes $2^{1/32.33} - 1 \approx 2.17\%$ per year of geometric outperformance. For a 37-year-old evaluating strategies over a practical 5-to-10-year horizon, the required annual hurdle remains **+6.1 to +9.6 pp/yr**, not +0.77 pp/yr.

3. **Long-History French Proxies vs Concentrated Stock Picking:**  
   The French screens show severe post-1994 decay: Big Momentum dropped from +3.12 pp/yr ($t = 3.56$) in 1927–1993 to +0.08 pp/yr in 1994–2026 and -0.97 pp/yr in 2013–2026 (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens9-power-and-regime.md:515-545`), while Operating Profitability (`H1`) returned +0.83 pp/yr post-1994 (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:52-60`). However, French portfolios are broad, **value-weighted** New York Stock Exchange (`NYSE`) tercile/decile indices docked by a flat 1.0%/yr (Big) or 3.0%/yr (Small) cost assumption (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:240-244`). They prove that broad passive factor tilts do not deliver `2x SPY`, but they are a diluted proxy for an equal-weighted, concentrated (8–15 stock) multi-factor sleeve with controlled turnover.

4. **Fairness of the Leverage (A23) Finding:**  
   On a consistent benchmark basis (against true `SPY` total return of 11.39% `CAGR`, gap +3.55 pp/yr over 2006–2026), the registered 1.3x arm (70% `SPY` / 30% `SSO`) delivered +1.32 pp/yr (`max DD` -66.4% vs `SPY` -55.3%), 1.5x delivered +2.37 pp/yr, and the post-hoc 2.0x arm (100% `SSO`) delivered +4.15 pp/yr (`max DD` -84.8%, trailing `SPY` in 37.5% of rolling 10-year windows) (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:258-281`). Two critical discounts apply:
   - **Interest-rate regime:** Average T-bill yield over 2006–2026 was 1.64%. At +2.5 percentage points higher funding (~4.14%, closer to current T-bills), the 1.3x arm's excess `CAGR` drops from +1.32 pp/yr to **+0.52 pp/yr** on a consistent basis (+0.80 pp/yr after tax against the artifact's taxed-NAV `SPY` baseline, `research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:269-290`). No funding-sensitivity run was ever executed for the 1.5x or 2.0x arms (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-phase1-checkpoint.md:235`).
   - **Embedded `SSO` structural drag:** Comparing the 1.3x and 1.5x `SSO` blends against a theoretical frictionless `SPY` position financed at T-bills (`beta_matched_pretax_cagr`), `SSO`'s 0.89% expense ratio, ~0.75% swap financing spread, and daily-reset volatility decay cost **-1.23 pp/yr** at 1.3x (12.71% vs 13.94%) and **-2.07 pp/yr** at 1.5x (13.76% vs 15.83%) (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:294-305`).

5. **Paper-vs-SPY Attribution and Hidden Cash-Sweep Tax Churn:**  
   Lens 3's decomposition of the -3.76 pp runtime shortfall from 2026-06-24 to 2026-11-04 (+1.07% vs `SPY` +4.84%: -2.46 pp pre-August idle cash, -0.85 pp unapplied Monster Beverage [`MNST`] 2-for-1 split on Alpaca paper, -0.15 pp uncredited `SPY` dividend, -0.29 pp residual; `docs/research/2026-11-04-lens3-paper-vs-spy-gap.md:34-50`) is thorough. Excluding `MNST`, the stock picks roughly broke even (~0.0 pp). However, both the synthesis and challenge understate a major live-design flaw for a **taxable** account: between 2026-06-24 and 2026-11-04, the paper trader executed **52 `SPY` cash-sweep trades** (20 buys and 32 sells) (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:448-455`) to fund and defund short-term stock picks. In a real taxable account, repeatedly selling and rebuying `SPY` every few weeks realizes short-term capital gains on the 85% core and triggers wash-sale disallowances across the book.

### The Single Most Likely Way the Synthesis Is Wrong (and How Much It Matters)
The synthesis's biggest blind spot is treating the **50 negative `WFO` test verdicts** as evidence about the underlying trading signals rather than evidence that the `WFO` evaluation harness was broken. In `research/programme/wfo-methods/2026-11-02-wfo-policy-synthetic.md` (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:757-766`), when a synthetic strategy with a **true Sharpe ratio of 1.0** and a 100% gate pass rate is fed into the repo's 17-fold `WFO` engine, the drawdown/loss circuit breaker still halts at least once in **87.5% of runs**, substitutes the fallback anchor in 34.1% of folds, and leaves **49.6% of runs permanently halted** at the end of the test. When gates pass 30% of the time, the anchor fallback runs in 86.8% of folds (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:761-766`). Worse, when `xs_momentum` was re-run after fixing the Deflated Sharpe Ratio (`DSR`) formula bug (`commits 90ef43ec`, `d17df13a`), the `WFO` gates rejected the candidate in **17 out of 17 folds**, so the reported +4.37 pp/yr `OOS` excess return ($t = 0.76$) was 100% the plain 12-1 momentum anchor fallback (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:238`, `research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens9-power-and-regime.md:398`). Because 48 of the 50 gated `WFO` rows saved no ungated or fixed-rule baseline (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:831-845`), the project has not actually tested most of its 50 stock-selection candidates on a clean, un-halted basis.

*How much would this matter?* Fixing the `WFO` harness will **not** rescue the current 9.4%-allocated stock sleeve (because of the 10x exposure dilution) and will **not** rescue high-turnover daily signals in a taxable account (because of the +2.42 to +5.05 pp/yr short-term tax drag). However, if an ungated, low-turnover cross-sectional signal earns +3 to +5 pp/yr at 100% equity weight (similar to the 12-1 momentum anchor's point estimate of +4.37 pp/yr over 2022–2026), removing the broken circuit breaker and the 85% `SPY` dilution could matter by **+2 to +4 pp/yr** at the book level—still short of +9.57 pp/yr on a 5-year bull window, but enough to beat `SPY` after tax if held for >12 months.

### Additional Findings Missed or Under-Emphasized by Synthesis and Challenge
1. **Partial-bar volume corruption in live `vbb` voting:** At 12:45 PM Pacific Time (`PT`) (15 minutes before the 1:00 PM `PT` equity close), `signal_runner.py` builds indicators using today's unfinished intraday daily bar (`docs/research/2026-11-04-lens6-live-vs-backtest-parity.md:45-52, 240-245`). The Volatility Breakout / Bollinger (`vbb`) voter requires `volume > 2.0 * SMA(volume, 20)`. Because the final 15 minutes of regular trading typically carry 15%–25% of full-day equity volume, comparing a 12:45 `PT` partial volume bar to a 20-day average of full-day volume systematically suppresses live `vbb` entry votes relative to daily-close backtests.
2. **Accidental `ohlcv` healing via stale symbol `EA`:** Because `trader.py` writes the unfinished 12:45 `PT` daily bar into the shared PostgreSQL `ohlcv` table (`docs/research/2026-11-04-lens6-live-vs-backtest-parity.md:256-268`), historical bars would remain permanently truncated if `refresh_universe_history()` only fetched from the latest date forward. Currently, `ohlcv` is only healed on subsequent days because delisted/acquired symbol `EA` (Electronic Arts, last bar 2026-08-11) drags `min(last_dates)` back to August on every run (`docs/research/2026-11-04-lens6-live-vs-backtest-parity.md:260-266`). Removing `EA` without fixing the incremental refresh logic will silently corrupt `ohlcv` with 12:45 `PT` partial bars.
3. **Alphabetical 3-name truncation (`signal_runner.py:100`):** Live `signal_runner.py` caps stock holdings at `max_stock_positions = 3` by sorting passing tickers alphabetically (`A-to-Z`) and taking the first three (`docs/research/2026-11-04-lens6-live-vs-backtest-parity.md:54-60, 248-252`), whereas `WFO` has no 3-name cap and the production replica ranks by trailing 20-day return (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens1-backtest-validity.md:145-152`).

  1. **Sample & Free Universe:** Extend evaluation to 1994–2026 ($T = 32.3$ years) under the Forward-Paper Standard Part A (`research/programme/evidence-standard/2026-10-23-forward-paper-standard.md:15-48`) using free point-in-time (`PIT`) S&P 500 historical constituency lists (`data/sp500_pit_constituents.csv`) paired with Tiingo/Yahoo daily history and free Securities and Exchange Commission (`SEC`) EDGAR fundamental filings (already partially ingested for `a05`/`a19`, `docs/research/2026-11-04-lens5-parameter-and-hypothesis-space.md:85-110`). Expanding $T$ from 5.24 years to 32.3 years cuts `SE` by $\sqrt{32.3 / 5.24} \approx 2.48\times$, dropping the standalone sleeve `MDE` ($t \ge 3$) from ~22.7 pp/yr to **~9.1 pp/yr** (and to **~6.8 pp/yr** at $t \ge 2$).
  2. **Tax-Aware Holding Period (>366 days):** Restrict entries/rebalances to an annual or buffered-quarterly schedule with a minimum 366-day hold on winning lots (unless a hard fundamental/delisting stop fires) so gains qualify for the 15%/20% long-term capital gains rate instead of 24%/37% ordinary income. This immediately saves **+2.0 to +4.5 pp/yr** in after-tax hurdle (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:434-441`).
  3. **Concentration & Signal:** Test an equal-weighted 10-to-15 stock sleeve (at 30%–100% sleeve weight in research so it is not masked by `SPY`) combining 12-1 cross-sectional momentum with EDGAR operating profitability/low accruals (the two factors with positive post-1994 drift in the French data: +0.83 pp/yr for `H1` Big HiOP and +4.37 pp/yr point estimate for the 12-1 anchor in 2022–2026, `research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:52-60`, `research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:238`).

Likelihood of clearing +1.0 pp/yr net since 1994 at Holm $t \ge 3$: **~15%–25%** (my estimate, given post-1994 factor compression). ## 5. Straight Talk for the Owner

Doubling `SPY`'s raw return over a 5-year bull market like 2021–2026 means turning +109% into +217%—an extra **+9.6 percentage points per year**, every year (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:157-168`). In a taxable account using only free data, **no tested strategy in this repository comes close to that over a 5-year window**, and it is almost certainly unreachable through S&P 500 stock picking alone.

Here is what is **known** from the numbers:
1. Your current paper trader cannot beat `SPY` by much because 85% of it *is* `SPY`, 5% is cash, and only 9% is in three stocks (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens9-power-and-regime.md:49-54`).
2. Rapid trading (25-day average holds) in a taxable account hands 2.4 to 5.1 percentage points per year to the IRS before you make a dime over `SPY` (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:434-441`).
3. The only tested path that beat `SPY` by +3.5 to +4.2 percentage points per year over 20 years was holding 2x leveraged `SSO`, which lost **84.8% of its value** in 2008–2009 and benefited from near-zero interest rates (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:258-281`).

Here is what is **assumed** vs **realistic**: assuming better indicator tuning on daily bars will double `SPY` is false. However, because the lab's walk-forward circuit breaker accidentally shut down 87.5% of good runs (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens2-verdict-audit.md:757-766`), a concentrated (10–15 stock), **12-month-plus hold** momentum-and-quality sleeve has a fair shot at **+1.5 to +4.0 percentage points per year** over `SPY`—and over 20 to 30 years of compounding, +2.3 percentage points per year is actually enough to end up with twice `SPY`'s wealth (`research/audits/root-cause-2026-11-04/2026-11-04-lens4-feasibility-and-ceiling.md:175-183`).
