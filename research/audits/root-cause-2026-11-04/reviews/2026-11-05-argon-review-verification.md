### 4(b) What is new beyond the synthesis, the challenge and the earlier reviews

1. **Cash-sweep tax churn.** The count (52 trades) is in Lens 4 only; the synthesis does not carry it. Turning it into a design question (the core SPY lot is not simply held) is useful. Unquantified.
