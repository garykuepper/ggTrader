**Production replica, resolved:** it is **inconclusive**, not "Reliable NO, high trust".
The recorded verdict stays exactly as the artifact gives it:

- `closure.verdict` is NO-GO (stage `pretax_fail`) for the primary window;
- the top-level `verdict.verdict` is INCONCLUSIVE, because the holdout stopped on tainted
  trades.

NO-GO is the correct reading of its registered bars (t -0.69 < 3; gap -0.64 < +1.0 pp/yr).
It cannot be called reliable, for two reasons:

1. Its MDE is 4.24 pp/yr, so it could not have detected the +1.0 pp/yr edge its own bar asks
   about.
2. It traded PARA, a misidentified security, 3 times without flagging it. The effect is
   unmeasured.

What it does show: it rules out a **book-level** excess above about 4.2 pp/yr at 80% power,
provided PARA proves immaterial. That is under half the 9.57 pp/yr book-level gap that the
2x-SPY target needs over the same window (Lens 9).

**Multiple testing:** 72 inventory rows carry test verdicts, in 11 families. Suppose every
tested idea had zero edge and the tests were independent:

- a one-sided t >= 2 bar would produce about 1.6 false "wins", with an 81% chance of at least
  one;
- a t >= 3 bar would produce about 0.10, with a 9% chance of at least one.

Most of the project's bars, though, had no significance threshold at all. They were point
estimates: Sharpe above SPY's, beat the 1.12 baseline, or gate counts.

**Gate machinery:** 50 of the 72 test verdicts rest on a gated WFO. Under the synthetic model,
the circuit breaker halts 87.5% of 17-fold runs even when the true Sharpe is 1.0. Three
artifacts let us drop the WFO or Holm part of the bar: month-end Treasury, the cross-asset
trend arm and the momentum Holm step. In all three the verdict is unchanged. Only the first
two are gated WFO rows; for the other 48 gated rows the question is **unknown**.

**Researcher freedom:** 18 dated revisions to gates, windows, bars and benchmarks are listed
below.

- Three revisions loosened the WFO gates within four days (2026-06-24 to 06-27), in the run-up
  to the 1.12 and 1.14 headlines.
- The October revisions tightened or were neutral.
- Every early GO rests on the loosened, leaky machinery. Every late NO rests on the tightened
  machinery, or on bars with little power at the +1 pp/yr scale.

## Classification rules

Fixed on 2026-11-04 before any row was classified. The same text is stored at
`classification_rules` in `docs/research/_lens2_sensitivity.json`, and the rules were applied
by one script (see Verification).

Lens 2 classification rules, fixed 2026-11-04 before any row was classified. Inputs are the committed upstream lens outputs only; no result value chooses a class except through these rules.

- R1 Verdict type, from the inventory verdict text through a fixed vocabulary: NO (a measured test reported fail, no edge, underperformance, did not reproduce, or no alternative passed); GO (a measured test reported pass, validated, beat, keep, shipped or done on evidence, or a model claim supported); TEST-INCONCLUSIVE (the test's own verdict is inconclusive or invalid); STATUS (feasibility, design, intake, literature, untriaged, component, operations, data-fix and audit labels). Every STATUS and TEST-INCONCLUSIVE row is classed inconclusive; STATUS rows are counted separately because they are not edge tests.

- R2 Data condition (Lens 1). Each contamination item gets a direction from a fixed table with evidence: anchor_leak, pit_universe, universe survivorship, ML-gate training leakage and event pre-entry returns favour the strategy (look-ahead or survivorship); spy_duplicates is unclear (it deflated strategy and SPY Sharpe alike); dsr_units, day_shift and tape identity faults are unknown. The condition is met for a NO if the tape was clean or every item favours the strategy (it lost despite the help), and for a GO only if the tape was clean. Lens 1 'unknown' with no items, and crypto rows (Lens 8 owns them), do not meet it.

- R3 Code condition (Lens 6). Met if Lens 6 says yes; if Lens 6 has no row or is unverifiable but Lens 7 independently recomputed the tested statistic from frozen raw inputs; or if Lens 6 says partly and the mismatch is outside the verdict's declared scope, logged as a named exception. Not met if Lens 6 is partly (otherwise), no, unverifiable or absent.

- R4 Recompute condition (Lens 7). Met if Lens 7 match is yes, or the row has no raw artifact and its claim is prose only (flagged as such). Not met for no, partial or cannot_locate.

- R5 Power condition (Lens 9), NO rows only. The effect a NO is used to rule out is the row's own pre-registered economic bar in pp/yr (+1.0 for the replica, momentum replays and French screens); if none, the owner's 2x-SPY target gap over the row's window from Lens 9 (target_required_cagr_gap_pp). Met only if Lens 9's MDE at t >= 3 (80% power) is known and at or below that effect. An MDE that Lens 9 could not compute means power is not established.

- R6 Classes. NO rows: 'unreliable NO (flawed test)' if Lens 6 says no and the row's claim or headline names the later-voided 1.12 baseline as its comparator, or if Lens 7 says the numbers do not recompute; otherwise 'reliable NO' if R2-R5 are all met; otherwise 'inconclusive', listing every unmet condition. GO rows: 'unreliable GO' if any contamination item favours the strategy, Lens 6 says no, or Lens 7 says no; otherwise 'reliable GO' if the tape was clean, R3 and R4 are met and the bar was pre-registered (Lens 7 status pre-registered or amended); otherwise 'inconclusive'.

- R7 'Rules out effects larger than' is Lens 9's MDE at t >= 3 in pp/yr where Lens 9 computed one, else 'unknown'. It is reported for every row, whatever its class, so that no NO is read as 'no edge of any size'.

## Multiple testing

**Counting rule** (stated before counting):

- A test is one inventory row whose verdict is a measured outcome, that is, type NO, GO or
  TEST-INCONCLUSIVE under rule R1. Status rows are not tests.
- Registered multi-hypothesis rows add their extra hypotheses: the factor screen's 4 add 3;
  momentum's 2 groups add 1.
- Parameter search inside a WFO (for example 48 combos x 17 folds for the ensemble,
  `docs/research/_rebaseline_corrected_tape_20260822.json` `core.table`) is not counted. The
  DSR gate is meant to deflate for it.
- Git is a cross-check only. The repository has 1,093 commits; 32 subjects contain "NO-GO".
  CLAUDE.md gives the strategy registry as 38 entries.

**Count:**

- 72 test rows: 53 NO, 16 GO, 3 TEST-INCONCLUSIVE.
- 76 hypotheses, including the registered multi-hypothesis rows.
- 11 families: ensemble and its levers 33; allocation and leverage 6; event and information
  signals 8; momentum 5; factor and anomaly screens 4; calendar and macro timing 4; macro
  approximations 3; leveraged ETF timing 2; pairs 1; the synthetic gate study 1; crypto 5.
  Family membership is in `multiple_testing.families` in the JSON.
- The 72 overstate the independent count. dual_momentum duplicates xs_momentum exactly
  (Lens 7). The July summary row aggregates other rows. Ensemble rows share one strategy and
  one window. So the effective number of independent tests lies between 11 (families) and
  72.

**What a "win" would mean by chance.** Assume every tested idea had zero true edge and the
tests were independent. This is a labelled assumption: real tests here are positively
correlated, which lowers the chance of at least one false win but not the expected count.

| Bar | N = 72 rows: expected false wins | P(at least one) | N = 11 families: expected | P(at least one) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| t >= 2, one-sided (p 0.0228) | 1.64 | 0.81 | 0.25 | 0.22 |
| t >= 3, one-sided (p 0.00135) | 0.10 | 0.09 | 0.015 | 0.015 |
| t >= 2, two-sided (p 0.0455) | 3.28 | 0.97 | 0.50 | 0.40 |
| t >= 3, two-sided (p 0.0027) | 0.19 | 0.18 | 0.03 | 0.03 |

What this means for a "win" in this project:

- **A single t >= 2 result would have been expected by chance alone.** At 72 tests, at least
  one false t >= 2 win is more likely than not (81%).
- **A t >= 3 result would have been informative.** The project's later bars (t >= 3 in
  `src/ggTrader/lab/significance.py:13-15`) keep the expected false-win count over the whole
  project near 0.1. A one-sided p of 0.00135 is the Bonferroni 5% level for about 37 tests
  (0.05 / 0.00135).
- **Most project bars had no significance threshold.** "Sharpe above SPY's", "beat the 1.12
  baseline" and gate counts were point estimates. Under zero edge, a strategy whose Sharpe
  is about SPY's passes "Sharpe above SPY's" about half the time (labelled assumption:
  symmetric noise). This applies to the GO rows (`cross-asset-static-80-20-sleeve`,
  `cross-asset-sleeve-holdout-2007-2011`, `stocks-core-spy-blend-keep`) and to most NO rows.
  For the GO rows the cross-asset artifact gives an outside check: its post-hoc bootstrap puts
  P(book Sharpe > SPY) at 0.898 for the pinned window and 0.9925 for the long one
  (`post_hoc.pinned.p_book_sharpe_gt_spy`, `post_hoc.long.p_book_sharpe_gt_spy` in

## Gate machinery

**What it does** (code at HEAD):

- **Gates.**
  - NDH passes when at least 85% of neighbours are profitable and Sharpe dispersion is no
    more than 0.20 of the peak. The dispersion cap is waived when density is 1.0
    (`src/ggTrader/lab/gates.py:57-117`).
  - DSR passes when the deflated Sharpe is at least 0.80 against the expected maximum Sharpe
    over the grid (`src/ggTrader/lab/gates.py:190-215`). Before `09f9f40` it compared an
    annualized Sharpe with per-day thresholds and "passed almost everything".
- **Fallback.** If either gate fails, or the system is halted, the fold deploys the anchor:
  the global minimum-drawdown parameters (`src/ggTrader/lab/wfo.py:747-752`).
- **Circuit breaker.** It halts when the trailing 4-window average WFE is below 0.25, or after
  two consecutive negative OOS Sharpes (`src/ggTrader/lab/wfo.py:79-125`).
- **Re-entry.** The system re-enters when 2 of the last 3 windows are clean: NDH and DSR pass
  and WFE is at least 0.5 (`src/ggTrader/lab/wfo.py:128-165`; changed from 2 consecutive in
  `d3d0c875`).

**Synthetic evidence** (`docs/research/2026-11-02-wfo-policy-synthetic.md` sections 5.1 to
5.4; it models the breaker bookkeeping with Bernoulli gates, not the real NDH/DSR):

- At a true Sharpe of 1.0 with gates always passing, P(at least one halt) is **0.875**. The
  mean anchor share is 0.341, and 49.6% of runs end halted.
- With gates passing 30% of the time, the anchor share is 0.868.
- At a true Sharpe of 0 the halt probability is 0.984; at 1.5 it is still 0.729.
- The two-consecutive-negative trigger alone gives 0.728 at Sharpe 1.0.
- Under these assumptions the breaker cannot separate a Sharpe-1 strategy from a Sharpe-0
  one. It halts both, and a halted fold reports the anchor's return, not the candidate's.

## Steelman

**The strongest evidence that the project's NO-GO verdicts are sound:**

- **Independent data, independent code, the same answer.** The three French screens use
  survivorship-free Ken French portfolios, not the project's tape, so none of the tape faults
  apply (Lens 1: clean). Lens 7 recomputed their statistics from the frozen raw files with
  its own parser, to 1e-12 for momentum. Over 754 months the factor t-statistics are 0.13 to
  1.34, and net issuance is 0.26. Every post-1994 point estimate is below +1.0 pp/yr: -0.62
  to +0.83 for factors, +0.08 / +0.20 for momentum, +0.48 for net issuance. These cannot be
  products of the five bugs.
- **The replica is negative on its own primary terms**, after all five fixes, on a pinned
  window, with PIT membership and next-bar fills:
  - point estimate -0.64 pp/yr CAGR, t -0.69;
  - 95% CI on mean excess [-2.93, +1.40] pp/yr;
  - against an exposure-matched passive book, +0.26 pp/yr (t 0.20).

  Unless PARA's three round trips were worth several pp/yr to the book, the replica excludes
  a book-level edge of target size (9.57 pp/yr needed; MDE 4.24).
- **Biases mostly helped the strategies.** Every leak whose direction is known (anchor, PIT,
  survivorship, pre-entry event returns) favours the strategy. Early results that carried
  them still fell short of SPY on CAGR. When the leaks were removed, results fell: core
  Sharpe went from 0.99 to 0.59 after `c9d1abe`, and the 1.12 headline did not reproduce

- **Corrections went the conservative way.** FOMC clustering lowered net t from 1.57 to 0.98.
  The DSR fix made the gates stricter. Holm control was pre-registered for the French

- **Where the gate machinery can be removed** (month-end, trend arm), the NO stands on the
  non-WFO criterion alone.

**The strongest evidence that some are not sound:**

- **Underpowered at the bar they enforce.** The one five-year SPY-excess test with a usable

- **Most NOs have no artifact.** 38 NO rows have no brief and most have no raw artifact

**Unknown, with the reason.** The exploratory table shows strict bars rejecting some positive

### 3. How many tests were run, and how does multiple testing change what "a win" would have meant?

**Answered.**

- 72 test rows (76 hypotheses with registered multi-hypothesis rows) in 11 families. Git has
  32 commit subjects containing "NO-GO" out of 1,093.
- Under zero edge and independence, a one-sided t >= 2 bar produces about 1.6 false wins
  (P(at least one) 0.81). t >= 3 produces about 0.10 (P 0.09).
- So a lone t >= 2 "win" anywhere in this project would have been expected by chance. A
  t >= 3 win would not.
- Point-estimate bars (Sharpe above SPY's) have no false-win control and pass about half the
  time under zero edge (labelled assumption).
- Within-family Holm was used only by the factor and momentum screens. Nothing controls
  across families.
